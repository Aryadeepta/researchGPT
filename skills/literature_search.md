# Literature Search Skill
## Goal
Conduct a focused search for state-of-the-art developments and IDENTIFY RESEARCH GAPS.
## Instructions
1. Analyze the topic to identify core search keywords.
2. Summarize key methodologies and findings from relevant literature.
3. **CRITICAL**: Explicitly list at least 3 things that remain UNKNOWN, UN-SOLVED, or perform poorly in current literature.
4. Your summary MUST be formatted to be used as a 'Novelty Gap Analysis' to guide the next research steps.
5. Provide a list of references.
