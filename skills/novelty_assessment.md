# Novelty Assessment Skill

## Objective
Evaluate a proposed research topic and workflow for high-impact novelty and technical feasibility.

## Assessment Criteria
1. **Novelty:**
   - Does this approach significantly deviate from established methods?
   - Is it addressing a gap in current literature?
   - Does it propose a unique combination of techniques?

2. **Feasibility:**
   - Are the required tools and computational resources realistic for an autonomous agent?
   - Is the proposed workflow logically sound and step-by-step?
   - Can the expected results be empirically verified within the project scope?

## Evaluation Output
- Provide a score (1-10) for both novelty and feasibility.
- If the score is below 8, provide concrete suggestions for improvement.
- If the score is 8 or above, conclude with "APPROVED".
